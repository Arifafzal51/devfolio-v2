# Essence Royale — Luxury Fragrance Startup

## Overview

Essence Royale is an artisanal luxury fragrance brand and direct-to-consumer digital commerce platform founded by Arif Afzal. In March 2025, the venture was awarded **₹10,00,000 in seed funding** under the Startup Bihar initiative by the Department of Industries, Government of Bihar, in recognition of its entrepreneurial promise and unit economics.

## Founder Responsibilities & Accomplishments

- **State Seed Funding:** Formulated and defended the comprehensive business model, unit economics, supply chain strategy, and financial projections before the state evaluation committee.
- **Digital Storefront Architecture:** Designed and built a responsive e-commerce storefront with intuitive perfume scent profile filters, high-conversion product pages, and secure checkout.
- **End-to-End Operations:** Directed supplier sourcing, fragrance formulation partnerships, packaging design, and logistics fulfillment across India.

## Impact & Recognition

- Recognized as an exemplary grassroots entrepreneurial initiative in Bihar.
- Showcased sustainable direct-to-consumer unit economics with strong customer retention.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Motion
- **Backend & Database:** Node.js, PostgreSQL
- **Design:** Figma (Design System & Brand Identity)
